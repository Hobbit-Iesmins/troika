THE WAY WE PLAY

Troika house rules

Get rid of every card. The last player holding cards is the Troika. Standard tables have 5 seats and one deck. Expanded tables have 9–12 seats and 2–4 decks; every player still gets nine cards.

  1. Three down. Three up. Three in hand. Play your hand first, then face-up cards, then blind cards one by one. No pre-game swaps.
  2. Lowest hand card opens. Ties go to the lowest numbered seat. Play an equal or higher rank. Suits don’t matter. You may play up to four cards of one rank in a single turn.
  3. Optional jump-in burns. If enabled by the host before creation, play one highlighted hand or face-up card out of turn to complete the chosen four-card burn and take the next turn. Blind cards cannot jump in.
  4. Keep three in hand. After playing, draw back to at least three while the deck has cards. Table cards stay locked until the deck and your hand are empty.
  5. 6 resets. Play it on any card. The next player can play any rank.
  6. 10 burns. Play it on anything. The whole pile goes into the hole and you play again.
  7. Four equal cards in a row burn. The host chooses Any four (suits ignored) or All four suits (♠ ♥ ♦ ♣ of one rank). Only an uninterrupted run of that rank counts; repeated suits can stay in that run until all four appear. Whoever completes the burn gets another turn. Otherwise, one play per turn.
  8. Can’t beat it? Pick up the pile and your turn ends. With face-up cards, choose a card to attempt: if it’s too low, take only your attempted card(s) plus the pile. Other face-up cards stay put.
  9. The blind finish. Try one face-down card. If it’s too low, take that card and the pile into your hand. Clear your hand before trying another.
