TROIKA — LOCAL CARD CLUB

1. Put this troika folder in MAMP's htdocs folder.
2. Start Apache in MAMP with PHP 8 or newer (PDO SQLite enabled).
3. Open http://localhost:8888/troika/ (use your MAMP Apache port).
4. Enter a nickname, choose your table mode and seat, then click Practice with bots.

To play with friends:
Create a table instead. Friends open http://YOUR-PRIVATE-IP:PORT/troika/
on the same local network, enter the table code, pick seats and get ready.
Then the host clicks Deal the cards.

Before creating the table, the host chooses:
- Standard: five seats and one deck.
- Expanded: 9-12 seats and two, three, or four decks.
  Twelve seats require at least three decks; the form handles this for you.
A round only needs two ready players. Add bots to fill more seats if you like.
The table keeps these settings for rematches. All house rules stay the same.

No Node.js, Composer, MySQL or internet connection needed to play.
Do not double-click index.html: open it through MAMP.
Full rules, setup and troubleshooting are in README.md.
